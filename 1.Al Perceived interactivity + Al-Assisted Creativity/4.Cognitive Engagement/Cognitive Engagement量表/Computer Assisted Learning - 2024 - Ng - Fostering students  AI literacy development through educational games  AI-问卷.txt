Table S2. Constructs that were measured in this study.
Constructs	Sub-constructs	Descriptions
Affective engagement 	Intrinsic motivation	It refers to students’ doing of AI learning activity for its innate satisfactions (Deci & Ryan, 2000).
	Self-efficacy of learning AI	It refers to a student’s belief in his/her capacity to execute behaviors necessary to produce specific performance attainments (Tomás et al., 2020).
	Confidence in using AI	It refers to a student’s trust in his/her own capacities and judgments, or belief that he or she can use AI to solve various problems (Dixon et al., 2020).
	Career interest	It refers to students’ preferences regarding their future work and study related to AI (Kier et al., 2014).

	Apply, evaluate and create AI	Higher-order thinking skills: Apply practical knowledge to evaluate, appraise, predict, design with AI applications in different scenarios.
	AI ethics	Students’ moral mindsets and critical knowledge/skills intended to inform the ethical and responsible use of AI.

Appendix S5. Recommendations for educators 
Reflecting on our experiences from the journey of this study, some recommendations are listed as follows:  
To support students’ AI literacy development, it is essential to consider meaningful activities (e.g., simulations, serious games, videos) to visualize complex AI concepts.
Game elements such as leaderboards and badges are effective means to motivate students and develop their confidence when they compete positively with their classmates. Moreover, games with levels of difficulties, feedback as dialogues and storyline are great ways to scaffold students’ AI literacy.
Gamification can enable students to sustain their learning motivation even after school beyond regular class time.
Students can develop basic AI understandings through gamification. Other pedagogies such as project-based learning and flipped classroom are suggested to enhance students’ higher cognition skills. 
AI ethics is an important issue. Gamification can foster students’ ethical mindsets through solving dilemmas and evaluating various AI applications. 
Game elements can be used to break down difficult contents into digestible pieces through task-based activities.
